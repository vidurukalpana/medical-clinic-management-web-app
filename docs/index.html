  • Paper registers at reception
  • Calling every patient about changes

With CareFlow

  • Book online in three steps
  • Follow the live queue from home
  • Check in with a QR code
  • Patients move their own booking

Privacy and security

No diagnoses, prescriptions or consultation notes.

No clinical records

Bookings and queues only.

Minimal details

Just a name and phone number.

Private codes

Shown once, stored as a hash.

Numbers, not names

The live queue shows numbers only.

Role-based access

Doctors see only their patients.

Secure sign-in

Argon2 hashing, expiring sessions.

Request limits

On sign-in, booking and chat.

Safe chat

Public clinic information only.
