# CareFlow Clinic

CareFlow Clinic is a small family medical centre offering general (GP) consultations for adults and children.

## Contact and location
- Address: No. 42, Lake View Road, Colombo 05, Sri Lanka.
- Phone (reception): +94 11 234 5678.
- WhatsApp (messages only): +94 77 123 4567.
- Email: hello@careflowclinic.lk.
- The clinic is on the ground floor, next to City Pharmacy, a five-minute walk from the Havelock Town bus stop.

## Reception hours
- The reception desk is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 18:00, for enquiries and check-in.
- Reception hours are not consultation hours. Each doctor has their own consultation hours, listed under Doctors below.
- The clinic is closed on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays.

## Fees and payment
- General consultation: LKR 2,500.
- Children under 12: LKR 2,000.
- Payment is taken at reception after the consultation, by cash, card or LANKAQR.
- Booking online is free. There is no charge for cancelling.

## Services
- General consultations for adults and children.
- Follow-up visits, medical certificates and referral letters.
- Blood pressure and blood sugar checks during a consultation.
- The clinic has no laboratory or X-ray. Doctors give referral letters to nearby labs when needed.
- The clinic does not handle emergencies. In an emergency, call 1990 (Suwa Seriya ambulance) or go to the nearest hospital emergency unit.

## Facilities
- Free parking for 10 cars behind the building. Street parking is also available.
- Wheelchair access and an accessible toilet.
- Air-conditioned waiting area, free Wi-Fi and a children's play corner.
- A pharmacy is next door.

## Booking a visit
- Patients book on the website using the "Book a visit" page.
- Booking needs only a name and phone number, plus a doctor and an available time.
- An account is not required. Patients can optionally create an account to see their bookings on the "My bookings" page.
- Each booking is a single consultation slot, usually 10 minutes long.
- Please arrive 10 minutes before your booked time.
- Patients can also phone reception to book.

## Managing a booking
- After booking, the website shows a private booking code once. Keep it safe.
- Use the booking number and the private code on the "Manage booking" page to view, reschedule or cancel a booking.
- Patients with an account can cancel or reschedule on the "My bookings" page.
- A lost booking code cannot be recovered online. Contact the clinic reception for help.

## On the day
- Check in at reception when you arrive. Reception adds you to the doctor's queue.
- Walk-in patients can be seen if the doctor has free slots that day.
- Bring any previous prescriptions, test reports and your NIC or passport.

## Privacy
- The website never asks for symptoms or medical details.
- Do not share medical details, phone numbers or booking codes in the chat.
